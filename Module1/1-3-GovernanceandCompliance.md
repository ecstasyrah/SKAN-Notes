# Information Security Governance

## The Purpose and Principles

The purpose of governance is to make sure that the organization **actively manages** what information security it does. Information security governance generally follows **enterprise or corporate governance processes**.

Corporate governance aims to ensure an organization does what it intends to do, based on these principles:

* **Policies:** Stating what the organization will do based on objectives, risk, and laws.
* **Responsibility:** Allocating people to ensure policies are followed.
* **Reporting and Checking:** Reporting on adherence and utilizing independent checks.
* **Exception Management:** Managing situations when things don't happen as intended.
* **Accountability:** Holding people accountable for exceptions.
* **Review:** Checking policies to ensure they remain appropriate.

---

## Structure and Leadership

At a corporate level, a general policy mandates an information security policy with named, responsible roles who **report** to an enterprise governance function.

Depending on the organization, policy development is the responsibility of the:
* **CIO** (Chief Information Officer)
* **CISO** (Chief Information Security Officer)
* **Chief Risk Officer**

This policy is developed based on input from **risk and compliance specialists**.

---

## The Information Security Committee

Policy implementation and management are undertaken by a **formal committee**, managed by the governance function leader (who handles agendas, minutes, and tracking).

This committee typically involves:
* **Members:** Senior GRC personnel, IT, Legal, and Risk leaders.
* **Duties:** Adopting policies, monitoring compliance, and dealing with exceptions.

---

## The Reality of Compliance

> **No organization actually exists where it will be able to adhere to 100% of its information security policy all of the time.**

Because of this reality, the compliance function must answer an important question:

> **Is the organization doing what it committed to when it approved the information security policy?**
